01 · Introduction

The everyday friction of parking

Parking search wastes time, fuel and attention. ParkSphere focuses on making availability visible before the driver reaches the parking area.

⏱

15–20 Minutes Searching

Time lost before the trip even begins.

⛽

Fuel Wasted

Circling blocks burns fuel and increases emissions.

⚠

Parking Fines

Illegal parking can quickly turn into costly penalties.

04 · User Roles & Workflows

Different users, one connected system

👤

Customer

Register, search, book, receive confirmation and view history.

  • Search available parking slots
  • View options and select a slot
  • Book and receive confirmation
🛠

Staff

Monitor bookings, update slot status, assist customers and report incidents.

  • Log in to operations dashboard
  • Monitor live bookings
  • Update slot status
⚙

Admin

Manage users, parking areas, floors, slots and bookings.

  • Manage users and permissions
  • Organize areas, floors and slots
  • Review bookings and reports
